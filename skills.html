What I bring
to a product team.

Product Design is at the center. Frontend development and data analytics are supporting perspectives that make the design work more grounded and more implementation-aware.

Primary Discipline
Product Design is how I solve complex problems.

Good product design isn't about how things look. It's about how things work for the people using them.

I work across the full design process — from understanding the problem and structuring the experience through to designing the interface, building the design system, and working with engineering through implementation.

The skills below are the ones I use to do that work.

Research & Discovery

User interviews, UX audits, competitive analysis, journey mapping. Understanding the problem before designing the solution.

Information Architecture & User Flows

UI Design & Interaction

How I Work

How I turn problems
into products.

01

Understand

Who are we building for? What are they trying to accomplish? What does the business need? What are the constraints?

02

Structure

User journeys, information architecture, workflow mapping. This is where complexity becomes manageable.

03

Design

Wireframes, interaction patterns, high-fidelity UI, responsive layouts, prototypes — validated with users.

04

Systemize

Design systems, component libraries, and reusable patterns. Products grow — design should grow with them.

05

Validate

Usability testing, stakeholder review, and iteration before engineering begins.

06

Ship

Close collaboration with engineering through implementation. The work doesn't end at handoff.

What Makes Me Different

Two perspectives that
strengthen the design work.

Most product designers work in Figma and hand off to engineering. I understand what happens on the other side of that handoff — and I can use data to investigate problems rather than guess.

Differentiator #1 — Technical Awareness

I understand what
happens after Figma.

My frontend development experience gives me working knowledge of HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and how designs are actually implemented. I understand responsive behaviour, component structure, and browser constraints.

This means I design with implementation in mind, collaborate with engineers more effectively, and reduce the gap between what was designed and what ships.

HTML5CSS3JavaScriptReactGit / GitHubResponsive Web
Differentiator #2 — Analytical Thinking

I don't ignore
the numbers.

My data analytics experience gives me another way to investigate product problems. I can query databases, build dashboards, and understand what the numbers are actually saying about how people use a product.

This helps me challenge assumptions, identify problems early, and support design decisions with evidence — not just intuition.
